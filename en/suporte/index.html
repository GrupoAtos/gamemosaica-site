Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet to play?

No. Mosaica works fully offline. The connection is only used, if you want, for Game Center (leaderboards and achievements) on iPhone and iPad.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Games, high scores and settings are stored locally. If you delete the app, that data is deleted too.

How does a game work?

You pick a clan and have 30 turns to score as many points as you can: growing cities, researching technologies, conquering rivals or raising your clan's Great Work. A quick tutorial appears in your first game and can be replayed from "How to play".

What is the Daily Challenge?

A map and clan drawn for the day, the same for every player. You can compare your score on the Game Center daily leaderboard.

How do I change the language?

The game follows your device language. To pick another one, open Settings from the game's title screen.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Mosaica is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Leaderboards and achievements don't show up

Check that you are signed in to Game Center in your iPhone or iPad Settings, under Game Center. Then open Mosaica again.
