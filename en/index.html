MOSAICA

Unite the tiles of the world

A turn-based strategy game in a world of isometric islands. Lead a clan, explore a map hidden by fog, found cities, research technologies and fight rival clans for territory. Every game lasts 30 turns and fits in a break.

Play now in your browser Meet the 12 clans

Free, right in your desktop or mobile browser. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

12 clans, 12 stories

Each clan has its own story, an exclusive unit, its own capital and a Great Work. Four of them are special: Automatons, Astrals, Specters and Colossi.

Lumens ✦

Harvest resources, build and train units. Every Lumen you spend is a choice.

Technologies

A three-tier tree to follow your own path: trade, war, sea or diplomacy.

Truces and pacts

Rivals negotiate, offer peace and break deals. Choose carefully whom to trust.

Seasons mode

The year turns during the game, changing the map and favoring different clans each season.

No internet, no ads

Play anywhere, in 8 languages. No ads and no in-app purchases.
