Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Summary: Mosaica does not collect, store on servers or share any of your personal data. It has no ads, tracking, analytics or sign-up.

Who we are

Mosaica is a game developed by Paulo Ricardo Pereira Serra ("we"). This policy covers the Mosaica app on iPhone, iPad and Android, the browser version and this website.

Data stored by the app

For the game to work, the app keeps on your device (or in your browser, for the web version): the current game, your high scores, clan stories already viewed and your preferences, such as language, difficulty and tutorial. This information never leaves the device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you remove the app or clear the site's data.

Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and does not use advertising identifiers. We use no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising, and this website uses no cookies.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your score and unlocked achievements to Game Center, a service operated by Apple. We do not receive your email, Apple Account or other account data; we only see what Apple shows publicly on leaderboards, such as the nickname you chose. Game Center is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device settings.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical access data, such as IP addresses, for security and operation of the service, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We have no access to those logs.

Email support

If you write to us, we use your address and message only to reply. We never share them and delete them when no longer needed.

Children

Because we collect no personal data, Mosaica can be used by people of any age without any of their information being collected.

Your rights (GDPR and LGPD)

We hold no personal data in our systems, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. For any question, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

Any update to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
